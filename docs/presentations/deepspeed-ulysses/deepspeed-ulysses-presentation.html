03 · CORE IDEASequence-parallel ↔ head-parallel
The trick

Change the layout, not the attention math.

Most of the layer is split by tokens. Attention is computed after an all-to-all regrouping by heads.

08 · CRITIQUEStrong result, real assumptions
Critical reading

The trick is powerful—but not free.

  • All-to-all needs a fast interconnect.
  • Parallel degree is capped by attention heads.
  • “Constant communication” assumes N and P scale together.
  • Newer Ring Attention / context-parallel methods are the obvious follow-up.
